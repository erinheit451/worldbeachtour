# Layer: Culture & legal
**Status:** idea
**Scores:** Uniqueness M · Leverage L · Cost M · Authority M · Revenue L

## What it is
Two sub-parts: cultural references (film locations, song references) and — the higher
value — the practical legal trio people actually search: nudity legality, dog rules,
bonfire/camping rules per beach.

## What it yields (DB columns)
`nudism`, `dogs_allowed`, `camping_allowed` (columns exist), fire/bonfire rules,
alcohol rules; film/song reference lists per notable beach. The legal trio is real
recurring search demand ("dog friendly beaches near me", "nude beach legal").

## Sources
OSM tags (`nudism`, `dog`), jurisdiction rule sets, local ordinance research,
film-location databases (IMDb/Wikidata), MusicBrainz/lyrics references for editorial.

## Depends on
Nothing hard. Legal rules need per-jurisdiction sourcing (accuracy-sensitive).

## Effort
Medium — the legal rules are the valuable, laborious part; culture refs are editorial
garnish for notable beaches.

## Open questions
- Accuracy/liability on legal claims — cite the ordinance, date it, "verify locally."
- Overlaps [sand-library](sand-library.md) no-collection list (protected-coast rules).
