Praia do Peneco is the western half of Albufeira's town seafront, paired with Praia dos Pescadores to the east, on Portugal's Algarve coast, about 38.5 km (23.9 miles) by road west of Faro. English Wikipedia lists it under a different official title, "Praia do Túnel (Peneco)," after the roughly 20-meter pedestrian tunnel cut through the cliff beside the old town's tourist office; Portuguese Wikipedia and the municipality itself favor "Praia do Peneco." Both names describe one beach, not two. The "Peneco" name comes from a large detached sandstone boulder — a leixão — sitting on the sand and resisting the cliff's general retreat behind it; such formations are called "penecos" locally, per Albufeira's own municipal beach page.

Three routes bring people down from the old town: the cliff tunnel itself, a stairway to the west, and a free public elevator, the Elevador do Peneco, in the Rossio area. At the western end, a promenade leads to the Xorino Grotto, where 13th-century legend holds that Moors sheltered here after the Christian conquest of Albufeira — a story repeated by both English Wikipedia and Portugal's national tourism board.

The beach carries an EU Blue Flag for the 2026 season and rates "1 – excellent" on the EU's bathing-water scale, with lifeguards on duty in season, loungers and parasols for hire, and good access for disabled visitors. Portuguese Wikipedia is candid that it also gets overcrowded in summer, with parking shortages and traffic, and that water quality has "not always" been good, though it says that has improved in recent years.

**Who goes.** The combination of central old-town access and a genuine sand beach draws a broad mix: families and casual swimmers using the elevator or tunnel, and visitors drawn specifically to the Xorino Grotto promenade and the Peneco rock itself. That rock is currently the subject of an unresolved safety question — a March 2026 drone survey found it fissured, and Albufeira's mayor said it may, in principle, be demolished. The full story of the crack, the erosion behind it, and the town's unresolved response is in the [environment deep dive](/beaches/praia-do-peneco/environment).
