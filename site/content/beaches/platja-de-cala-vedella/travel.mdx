# Cala Vedella Travel Guide

Cala Vedella sits in the municipality of Sant Josep de Sa Talaia, on Ibiza's west coast. Sant Josep's own tourism site puts it 22 km by road from Eivissa (Ibiza Town) and 9 km by road from Sant Josep village itself. The WBT dataset's straight-line figures, measured from the beach's centroid, are 13.7 km to Ibiza Airport and 5.1 km to Sant Josep de sa Talaia — shorter than the road distances because they cut directly across the terrain rather than following it.

## Getting There

**By road:** The recommended route is the EI-700 out of Sant Josep de Sa Talaia toward Sant Antoni, following signs to the cove. The official access point is a left turnoff on the outskirts of Sant Josep, at kilometer 13 of the Eivissa-Sant Josep road, along a paved road.

**By bus:** A regular bus service runs during the summer months, starting in Ibiza Town and routed via Sant Josep de Sa Talaia.

**By boat:** The nearest marina is Puerto Deportivo de Coralmar, 7 km from the beach.

## Parking

Parking behind the beach holds 250 cars, including a small allocation of bays reserved for disabled drivers displaying a European blue badge. The sheet does not give current pricing or occupancy data for this parking, so plan for a busy summer lot rather than a guaranteed space.

## Safety

The main beach is patrolled by lifeguards during the summer/high season only; outside that window there is no coverage. Swimming and snorkelling are calm most of the year, but in some years the cove sees infestations of stinging jellyfish — worth checking locally before a family swim day. The access road itself is not free of incident: on 10 September 2026, emergency crews recovered a car that had rolled over after leaving the Cala Vedella access road overnight, with no serious injuries reported.

Parking has also become a flashpoint nearby. An August 2025 report found that a parking crackdown at neighboring Cala d'Hort pushed illegally parked cars onto the Cala Vedella access road, with the Guardia Civil and local police disputing which of them was responsible for enforcing it — worth knowing if the roadside looks crowded or informally parked.

## Costs

The research available for this page does not include priced beach services — sunbed or parasol rental rates, or restaurant menu prices at Vadella Beach Bar or Ca Na Sofía. The beach itself has no entrance fee. Budget for parking, food at the two beachfront restaurants (or in Sant Josep village), and transport; specific figures aren't available to quote reliably here.

## Behind the Calm

Cala Vedella's tranquil reputation sits on top of a residential development that has never been fully regularized since a 1973 plan — a story covered in full in [the fifty-two-year file behind the beach](/beaches/platja-de-cala-vedella/history).
