# Getting to Praia da Adraga

Praia da Adraga sits near the village of Almoçageme, in the parish of Colares, Sintra municipality — about 3.3 km from Colares itself. The nearest airport is Cascais Airport (CAT), 14.2 km away in a straight line; Lisbon Airport (LIS), Portugal's main hub, is about 30 km (19 miles) away, also straight-line. Road distance and drive time from Lisbon were not confirmed in any source used for this page.

Wikipedia describes the beach as hard to reach by public transportation, despite having an on-site restaurant and "a very good emergency service system." No bus route, parking operator, or fare information turned up in the research behind this page — plan for a car, and treat any route you find independently as unverified by this guide.

## Seasons

Adraga's 2026 bathing season and Blue Flag period both run 30 May to 13 September. Outside that window there is no confirmed lifeguard coverage. For surfers, December is statistically the best month at this point break, with clean, surfable conditions about 41% of the time and blown-out conditions the other 56%; Surf-Forecast otherwise rates the break as fairly consistent year-round.

Restaurante Adraga, the one confirmed business at the beach, keeps winter hours (October–May) of Wednesday–Sunday, 12:30–21:30, closed Monday and Tuesday; in summer it opens daily except Tuesday.

## Safety

Sintra's municipal council warns that the ground around the Fojo da Adraga carries added risk from slippery footing, strong wind, fog, rough sea, and high tide — plan any cliff scramble around the tide table, not the forecast. The point break itself carries two flagged hazards: rocks (both Surf-Forecast and Wannasurf) and localism (Wannasurf). The sea caves on the beach's left side are only safely accessible around low tide; the tide, not the weather, decides whether a visit there ends well — see the honest reckoning on the main guide for what happens when it isn't checked.
