# Praia da Adraga

Praia da Adraga is a wild, cliff-backed North Atlantic beach near the village of Almoçageme, in the parish of Colares, Sintra municipality, Lisbon district, Portugal. It sits within the UNESCO-listed Sintra Cultural Landscape protection zone, about 3.3 km from Colares and roughly 14.2 km (straight-line) from Cascais Airport.

The beach is defined by what surrounds it as much as by the sand itself. On the southern escarpment, the Pedra de Alvidrar drops almost vertically into the ocean — local tradition held that young men proved their courage climbing it. About fifty meters inland from the cliff, the Fojo da Adraga is a natural well roughly 90 meters deep, carved by rainwater and wave action into a labyrinth of caves and fissures; Roman-era legend held a Triton's conch echoed from its bottom. At low tide, the sand connects on to neighboring Praia do Cavalo.

In the water, Adraga is a point break — sandy-and-rock bottom, hollow and fast — rated by Surf-Forecast as an exposed beach-and-reef break with fairly consistent surf year-round. Both Surf-Forecast and Wannasurf flag rocks as a hazard, and Wannasurf adds localism. December is statistically the best surf month, clean about 41% of the time.

**Who this beach is for.** Surfers who don't mind rocks and localism; photographers drawn to the cliffs, caves and Pedra de Alvidrar; anyone willing to accept a beach with one restaurant and no other services. Wikipedia notes it is hard to reach without a car, and this is not a beach for casual, crowd-following sunbathers.

Adraga carries a Quercus "Golden Quality Beach" award for its bathing water and Blue Flag status through Portugal's 2025 national list, with a 2026 season running 30 May to 13 September. In 2003, readers and journalists of The Sunday Times ranked it the third-best beach in Europe — the only Portuguese beach on that list.

The land backing the beach is now the site of a multi-year ecological restoration project, bought from a bankrupt developer in 2021 — [the full story is in the environment deep dive](/beaches/praia-da-adraga/environment).
