# Getting to Olympic Beach

Olympic Beach (Olympiaki Akti) sits on Greece's Thermaic Gulf coast, in the Katerini municipality of Pieria, Central Macedonia — about 7.5 kilometers from Katerini's city center and roughly 3 kilometers south of the larger neighboring resort of Paralia. The nearest international airport, Thessaloniki (SKG), is 45 kilometers away in a straight line, but the actual drive is considerably longer: Wikipedia's infobox for the settlement lists the distance as 107 kilometers by road, because the route runs down through or around Thessaloniki before joining the A1/E75 motorway south. Don't plan a trip around the 45-kilometer figure — that's a straight-line measurement, not a driving distance.

## By Air

**Thessaloniki International Airport "Macedonia" (SKG)** is the practical hub for Northern Greece. From SKG, the route to Olympic Beach runs via the A1/E75 motorway, which has dedicated Katerini North and Katerini South interchanges — the Katerini South interchange connects directly to Olympiaki Akti, per Wikipedia's motorway description. Two motorway tunnels, the Filippos Tunnel (1,095 m, southbound) and the Alexandros Tunnel (1,100 m, northbound), sit on this stretch between Thessaloniki and Katerini. This write-up did not find a verified, current fare or exact drive-time figure for this route and won't invent one; budget more time than the straight-line distance suggests, since the road distance (107 km) is more than double it.

## By Bus (KTEL)

**KTEL Pierias** (ΚΤΕΛ Πιερίας, the regional coach operator, tel. 23510 23313 / 23510 46720) runs services within Pieria, including routes serving Katerini and its coastal settlements. This write-up could confirm KTEL Pierias' Katerini–Elassona schedule directly (departing Katerini 14:30, returning from Elassona 16:15, about 1.5 hours) but could not independently verify current Thessaloniki–Katerini or Katerini–Olympiaki Akti fares and timetables; confirm directly with the operator or at the Katerini KTEL station before relying on a specific departure time.

## By Train

**Katerini railway station (OSE)** is 7.1 kilometers from Olympic Beach and serves the Athens–Thessaloniki main line, including InterCity trains and the Proastiakos suburban service to/from Larissa. An express electric service named the **Silver Arrow** launched on 20 May 2019, running Athens to/from Thessaloniki with two intermediate stops (Lianokladi and Larissa) in about four hours; it does not stop at Katerini. From Katerini station, onward transport to the beach itself (taxi, local urban bus, or the settlement's local bus connecting Katerini city to Olympiaki Akti) is needed to cover the remaining distance.

## By Car

The A1/E75 motorway is the direct route from both Thessaloniki (north) and Athens/Larissa (south), with the Katerini North and Katerini South interchanges serving the coast. Wikipedia's infobox lists the settlement as 70 km from Thessaloniki and 434 km from Athens — both appear to be road-network figures given via the infobox rather than straight-line distances, consistent with how the same infobox handles the 107 km airport figure.

June is the calmest month on record; January is the roughest, though even January's mean (0.57 m) is mild by open-coast standards — this is a sheltered gulf, not an exposed Aegean shore. The single largest modeled day in the dataset reached 1.16 meters. Summer (June–September) is the conventional Greek beach season and, per the settlement's tourist infrastructure — hotels, rental rooms, bungalows, and a campground, per Wikipedia — is when the resort actually operates at capacity.

## Where to Stay

Olympiaki Akti's accommodation base is hotels, rental rooms, bungalows, and a campground directly along the waterfront, per the settlement's Wikipedia description; the neighboring, larger town of Paralia (3 km north, about 928 year-round residents) offers a wider range of lodging and dining. This write-up did not find verifiable, named, current listings for either town's specific hotels or guesthouses, so none are named here — check current booking platforms directly rather than relying on invented specifics.

## Safety

The EU rates the bathing water here "1 – excellent" (site ELBW099047006101), and the dataset behind this page records zero shark incidents for the area. The Blue Flag, a European eco-certification tied to water quality, safety information, and beach management, has been awarded to Olympiaki Akti every year except 1998, according to the Greek-language Wikipedia article — no source consulted here explains that single gap year. Wave heights are modest year-round (0.2–0.57 m mean by month), consistent with a semi-enclosed gulf rather than an open, swell-exposed coast.

## Local Notes

- **The name outdates the place, not the other way around.** Olympic Beach as a settlement dates to 1980 and as a name to 1986; treat "ancient" or "mythological" framing of the beach itself skeptically — see the history spoke on this page for the full account.
- **Two distances, two numbers.** Thessaloniki airport is 45 km away by straight line, 107 km by road. Mount Olympus's summit is about 26 km away by straight line — visible, not walkable from the sand.
- **Confirm bus timetables directly.** KTEL Pierias (tel. 23510 23313) operates regional coach services in Pieria; this write-up could not verify current Thessaloniki–Katerini fares or frequencies and did not invent figures for them.
- **The Katerini South motorway interchange goes straight to the beach.** If driving the A1/E75, this is the interchange to take for Olympiaki Akti specifically, rather than Katerini North, which serves Paralia and the city center.
