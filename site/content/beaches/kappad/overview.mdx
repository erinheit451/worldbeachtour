Kappad Beach is a quartz beach on the Arabian Sea, about 16 kilometres north of Kozhikode (Calicut) in Kerala and roughly 10 kilometres from the railway town of Koyilandy. Its Malayalam name is Kappakadavu. A rocky headland topped by a temple breaks one end of the shoreline, and the Korapuzha backwaters run behind the sand. The water is calm for most of the year, with a mean wave height near a third of a metre.

## Why this beach matters

A few steps back from the sand stands a low stone pillar inscribed "Vasco da Gama landed here, Kappakadavu, in the year 1498." The Kerala Department of Archaeology notified it as a protected monument in 1975, and it is the reason the beach is on the tourist map. Da Gama's arrival on the Malabar coast is a genuine hinge of world history — the opening of the sea route between Western Europe and the Indian Ocean.

The monument's precise claim, though, is contested. The voyage's only eyewitness log, the *Roteiro*, records that on 20 May 1498 the fleet anchored about a league and a half offshore after mistaking Kappad for Calicut, and that da Gama himself first went ashore on 28 May at Pantalayani Kollam, further south. Historian M. G. S. Narayanan has argued that the landing was near Koyilandy, not at Kappad, and that the error has never been officially corrected. The stone and the source disagree — which is the most interesting thing about the beach.

Kappad was one of India's first eight Blue Flag beaches in October 2020 and has held the Foundation for Environmental Education's certification, judged against 33 environmental and safety criteria, for six consecutive years to November 2025. A 2007 state beautification worth about 1.5 crore rupees added a corniche, a park, restrooms and a restaurant. The sand is unremarkable quartz, the sea is gentle outside the June–August monsoon, and the beach faces west, so the set piece is the sunset straight into the Arabian Sea.

## Most relevant lenses

**History** — The stone and the logbook: the disputed 1498 landing, what the *Roteiro* actually records, and why Kappad kept a claim its primary source contradicts. This is the beach's defining story.

**Travel** — Getting here from Kozhikode or Koyilandy, the calm winter season versus the monsoon, honest lodging options, and the Korapuzha backwater approach.
