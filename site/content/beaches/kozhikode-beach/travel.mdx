# Kozhikode Beach Travel Guide

Kozhikode Beach sits on Kerala's Malabar Coast, reached from the city via four separate road overbridges.

## Getting There

<DataCard label="Distance to Airport (straight-line)" value="24.4" unit="km" />

**By air.** Calicut International Airport (CCJ) serves the city. The Kozhikode district government's own tourism page states the airport is about 29 km from the beach and the railway station about 3 km — road-route figures, not the straight-line distance above; no official source confirms drive time or taxi fare for either route.

**By road.** The beach is accessible through four road overbridges connecting it to the rest of Kozhikode city.

**By rail.** The district tourism page puts Kozhikode railway station about 3 km from the beach.

## Safety

No official source publishes lifeguard staffing, designated swimming zones, or beach-closure protocols specific to Kozhikode Beach. In the same district, four people, including two women, drowned off Thikkodi beach — a different location about 36 km north — on January 27, 2025, after being swept away by strong waves; the rescue was carried out by local people and fishermen rather than official lifeguards. On the night of October 15-16, 2025, the sea receded roughly 200 metres along a 1.5 km stretch of South Beach; officials called it a rare tidal phenomenon after CMFRI scientists and the local taluk tahsildar inspected the site.

## Food

Vendors along the promenade sell kallummakaya (fried mussels), banana chips, quail eggs, and ice cream. For a sit-down meal or Kozhikkodan Halwa — a maida-flour, ghee, and jaggery-or-sugar sweet linked to Arab spice traders and, by legend, first made for the Zamorin of Kozhikode — head to SM Street (Mittai Theruvu), a 2.1 km market street in the city center.
