# Vodarevu Beach Travel Guide

Vodarevu has no tourist infrastructure of its own — no hotels, no ticketed access, no organized transport hub — so getting there and staying nearby both run through Chirala, the town about 6-8 km away (sources disagree by 2 km; see the overview for why).

## Getting there

**By air.** The nearest airport is Vijayawada, about 100 km from Vodarevu by a local village-profile source. No source consulted for this page gives a drive time or confirms a direct road route beyond the highways below, so budget conservatively for a roughly two-hour-plus drive.

**By rail.** Chirala and Ipurupalem railway stations are the nearest to Vodarevu. Neither the exact walking or taxi distance from these stations to the beach itself, nor current train schedules, appear in the source record for this page — check Indian Railways directly before relying on a specific service.

**By road.** National Highway 216 passes through Chirala, connecting Ongole and Kattipudi; NH544D is also listed as reaching the area. VRS & YRN College of Engineering & Technology, on Vodarevu Road in Chirala, is a fixed local landmark marking the route in from town. Beyond the highway numbers themselves, no source gives current drive times from specific cities, so plan the final approach with a local map rather than a fixed itinerary.

## Best time to visit

The recommended visiting season is October to March, per Outlook Traveller — the cooler part of the year on this stretch of coast. That said, both fatal drowning incidents on record for Vodarevu (2023 and 2025) happened in October, squarely inside this "recommended" window, so treat the season as a guide to comfortable weather rather than to water safety. The [currents deep dive](/beaches/vodarevu-beach/surf) covers what that distinction actually means in the water.

## Where to stay

Vodarevu itself has no overnight stay options. A 2017 Outlook Traveller account points visitors instead to two resorts in Chirala: Sea Breeze Beach Resort and Palm Coast Beach Resort. No current, non-stale pricing or confirmation that either resort is still operating turned up in this research pass — confirm directly before booking rather than relying on a nearly decade-old account.

## Safety

Vodarevu's water has killed visitors more than once: a Hyderabad woman in October 2023, and three VIT-Amaravati students (with two more people missing) in a single day in October 2025, all in what looked like ordinary, shallow water. No source describes a lifeguard service or a formal warning-flag system here. The full account — what happened, why the water is more dangerous than it looks, and what that means for a visit — is in [the currents deep dive](/beaches/vodarevu-beach/surf); read it before deciding whether, or how, to go in the water.

Beyond the water itself, Vodarevu is a working fishing village rather than a tourist site: boats, nets, and crews are active infrastructure, not backdrop. The source record for this page contains no information on medical facilities near Vodarevu.

## A quieter alternative

Ramapuram Beach, also in Chirala mandal, is described by the Bapatla district government portal as one of the area's cleanest and most peaceful beaches — worth knowing about if Vodarevu's weekend crowds, or its water, give pause.
