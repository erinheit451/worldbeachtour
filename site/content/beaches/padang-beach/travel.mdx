# Pandawa Beach Travel Guide

The mapping data behind this page's file names it "Padang Beach," but the beach itself, and every travel resource covering it, calls it Pandawa Beach — Pantai Pandawa. It sits in Kutuh village on Bali's Bukit Peninsula, reachable only by a single paved road cut through limestone cliffs in 2012. There is no walk-in or boat-only access documented; the road is the way in.

## Getting there

<DataCard label="Nearest airport" value="Denpasar I Gusti Ngurah Rai (DPS)" unit="10.9 km straight-line" />
<DataCard label="Nearest city" value="Bualu" unit="6.1 km straight-line" />

Both figures above are straight-line distances from this page's location dataset, not road distances — the Bukit Peninsula's cliff-hugging roads add real driving time. Travel guides covering the beach cite roughly a 1-hour to 1.5-hour drive from Kuta, Seminyak, or Canggu, depending on traffic; expect a similar range from DPS airport, which sits near the same Kuta-area road network. There is no confirmed public transit route to the beach; a scooter, car, or ride-hail (Gojek, Grab) is the practical option.

The access road ends in a parking area at the top of the descent into the cove. Parking runs Rp2,000 for a scooter and Rp5,000 for a car. From the bottom of the road, a right turn leads to the developed tourist strip; a left turn leads to a quieter, locally run stretch of beach near the surf break.

The developed side of the beach adds facility rentals on top: beach umbrellas and chairs, paragliding tandem flights from Bukit Timbis, and jet ski rentals, none of which this page has verified prices for. Food stalls on the developed strip sell standard Indonesian dishes (fried rice, noodles, grilled skewers) at typical Bali beach-tourism prices; the quieter, local-restaurant side near the surf break is generally cheaper.

## Best time to visit

<WeatherWidget />

Bali runs on two broad seasons: a dry season from roughly April through October and a wetter season from November through March, with the wettest months typically December through February. This dataset's wave model, drawn from Open-Meteo's ERA5 marine reanalysis for 2023–2024, shows the biggest average wave heights in August (2.25 m) and the calmest in November (1.53 m), with an annual mean of 1.91 m and a typical wave period of 11.8 seconds — figures that describe the open coastline off the Bukit Peninsula, not necessarily the sheltered water inside Pandawa's two headlands, which is calmer by design.

Because the beach's fame is tied to tour-bus tourism rather than surf conditions, crowding follows visitor patterns more than swell: arrive early in the day to beat the tour groups that fill the developed side by late morning.

## Safety

<DataCard label="Shark incidents" value="0" unit="recorded, this dataset" />

No shark incidents are recorded for this location in the dataset behind this page. The regional wave model flags this stretch of coastline as exposed with rip-current risk; that reading applies to the open Bukit Peninsula coast generally, and the enclosed cove behind Pandawa's headlands is the calmer water most visitors actually swim in. Wikipedia describes surfing at the beach itself as not ideal, so it does not draw the surf crowds — or the surf-specific hazards — that nearby Uluwatu breaks do.

IBTrACS's 50-year tropical storm archive logs 2 cyclone tracks passing within range of this location, one reaching Category 4 intensity. Bali sits at the edge of the belt where these systems typically form and rarely takes a direct hit; this reflects tracked storms passing at a distance, not confirmed landfalls on this beach.

## Practical notes

Beyond the beach itself, sarongs and other temple-appropriate dress are not required here — Pandawa is a beach, not a temple site — but the wider Bukit Peninsula includes temple areas (notably around Uluwatu, further west) where modest dress applies. Bring cash: the entrance fee, parking, and most vendor stalls on the developed side are cash-based, consistent with typical roadside tourism infrastructure on this part of Bali.
