# Kollam Beach Travel Guide

Kollam Beach sits at Thamarakulam in Kollam city, on Kerala's Arabian Sea coast, about 3 km from the city center -- roughly a 10-minute auto-rickshaw or taxi ride, per local visitor accounts. There is no entry fee for the beach itself, though visitors may pay separately for activities such as boating, surfing, fishing, or the adventure park.

## Getting there

The nearest airport is Trivandrum International Airport (TRV) in Thiruvananthapuram. The dataset behind this page places it about 56.8 km away in a straight line; road-distance estimates from a third-party visitor guide conflict with each other, giving 66 km in one place and about 70 km in another, so treat both as rough estimates rather than a settled figure.

Kollam Junction Railway Station is the nearest major rail stop, and the beach's own access figures put the nearest railway station 2.4 km away, the nearest bus station 3.1 km away, and the nearest ferry terminal another 3.1 km away. No source behind this page gives current fares for any of these connections, so budget for local transport on the day rather than relying on a fixed price here.

## Safety in the water

Kollam Beach carries a high hazard rating and is patrolled by lifeguards, a response that dates to 2005, when lifeguards were first stationed here; by July 2015 it was one of only three beaches in south Kerala, alongside Kovalam and Varkala, with a lifeguard outpost. Visitor advice is specific: avoid swimming or surfing in deep or rough water, especially at high tide or during the monsoon season. The water near shore runs deep with turbulent waves close in -- enough that a 2023 government-backed plan proposed reshaping the beach itself to reduce the risk; what is and isn't confirmed about that plan today is covered in the [history deep dive](/beaches/kollam-beach/history).

## Beyond the beach

Ashtamudi Lake, Kerala's backwater destination known for houseboat cruises, is about a 10-minute drive from Kollam Beach, making the beach a practical base for travelers heading into the backwaters rather than a backwater destination itself. Seafood is the reliable food option near the coast: karimeen fry, prawn curry, fish moilee, and crab masala are the specialties visitor guides point to, though no source here confirms a specific restaurant, price, or address, so ask locally rather than expecting a fixed list of vendors.

## Practical notes

Kollam Beach itself has no fixed entry fee, though nearby Mahatma Gandhi Park has historically charged a small entry and parking fee under past maintenance contracts -- figures too old to treat as current pricing. Bring cash for small local transport and food, expect variable water conditions rather than a calm, tourist-groomed shoreline, and treat any specific bus, train, or ferry fare quoted to you locally as more current than anything written here.
