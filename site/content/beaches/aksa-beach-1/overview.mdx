# Aksa Beach

Aksa Beach is a weekend beach and vacation spot in Aksa village, part of Malad, in Mumbai's northwestern suburbs, on the Arabian Sea. Wikipedia places it at 19°10′34″N, 72°47′43″E, bounded at one end by INS Hamla, an Indian Navy base, and at the other by a smaller beach called Dana Paani. The beach falls under the governance of the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC/MCGM). The East Indian community, the Panchkalshi or Somvanshi Kshatriya Pathare, and Koli fishing families are named as the area's native population.

## What it is

Aksa draws heavy weekend crowds from across Mumbai, and Wikipedia describes it as one of the cleanest beaches in the city, a description the same page complicates with its own account of shifting sand and strong currents. Sources do not even fully agree on Aksa's own neighborhood: Wikipedia places it close to Marve Beach, while Mumbai's own community wiki places it close to Malvani instead. Both agree it is the one and only Aksa Beach; a Wikipedia disambiguation page confirms no other place carries that exact name.

The beach's most consequential recent history is not scenic. It is a fight over a concrete seawall and promenade the Maharashtra Maritime Board built along roughly 600 metres of the shore starting in 2022, defended in front of an environmental tribunal for more than two years, and which began failing structurally before that tribunal ruled it illegal in September 2025. That story, including the government reports, the activists' objections, and what happened when the sea and the courtroom reached their verdicts around the same time, is told in full in the linked deep dive below.

## Safety, briefly

Aksa's water carries a real and long-documented risk to swimmers, separate from the seawall dispute: strong currents, shifting sand, and posted warning signs that have not stopped drowning incidents from recurring over multiple decades, including one as recent as June 2026. That record is reported in full further down this page's honest reckoning rather than repeated here; treat it as a governing fact about the beach, not a minor caveat.

## Who Aksa is for

Aksa is a working weekend beach for Mumbai residents rather than a curated tourist destination: fishermen, families, and evening crowds rather than resort infrastructure. It has also become an active filming location, most recently for scenes shot for the upcoming film *Ramayana*, starring Ranbir Kapoor, Sai Pallavi, and Yash, reported in August 2026. The same reporting that covers the film shoot repeats the same warning that runs through every other source on this beach: strong waves and rip currents, and official advice not to go in deep.
