Sunset Beach sits on English Bay in Vancouver's West End, between Coal Harbour and downtown to the east, Stanley Park to the northwest, and Kitsilano across False Creek to the southwest. It sits at the edge of the downtown core — walkable for most visitors already in the city — and is roughly 10 km in a straight line from Vancouver International Airport (YVR). No source in this record gives a road or transit distance to the airport, so treat any specific drive-time or transit-time figure you see elsewhere with caution.

## Getting there

The beach is bisected by Vancouver's seawall, a multi-use path for walking, cycling, and skating that connects it to the Vancouver Aquatic Centre site and the Burrard Street Bridge, and on to Stanley Park and False Creek in either direction. A False Creek Ferries dock serves the beach directly. On-site parking exists but is limited enough that during the annual April 20 gathering, the city closes the Sunset Beach parking lot from Saturday evening through Sunday, restricting vehicle access until Easter Monday.

## Seasons

Lifeguards are posted only between May and September. The water itself stays calm most of the year — marine reanalysis data for this stretch of English Bay characterizes it as gentle swimming water year-round, slightly rougher in winter than in summer. That makes Sunset Beach a calm-water beach by ocean standards in any season — though "calm" describes wave height, not water temperature or bacteria counts.

Two dates change what the beach feels like. Every April 20, an unofficial cannabis gathering fills the park; the city now stations park rangers, VPD officers, and protective services here for it, and closes the parking lot for the weekend. And most summers, Sunset Beach is one of the best places in the city to watch the fireworks over English Bay — in 2026, a single night on July 31, a 25-minute show launched from a barge in the bay at 10 p.m., after the traditional three-night competition was cancelled the year before over a funding shortfall.

## Safety

The most concrete safety issue on record here is water quality, not surf or currents. Sunset Beach has been closed to swimming for high E. coli counts at least twice: in August 2018 (alongside English Bay and Jericho Beach) and again in July 2022, when repeat samples came back at more than double the province's single-sample safety limit at the time. Vancouver Coastal Health's monitoring system exists because heavy rain runoff, sewage system overflows, leaking septic tanks, and boat discharge all feed E. coli into the bay; the agency tightened its own trigger for an investigation in 2023, so a closure today can be prompted by a lower reading than it would have been a few years ago.

Outside of water quality, no source in this record gives a shark-incident count above zero, current speeds, or drowning data specific to this beach.

## Costs

Sunset Beach itself is free — no entrance fee, and there is no confirmed on-beach concession vendor name to check for prices. The nearest confirmed food options are inland: Babylon Cafe on Denman Street, called out specifically for beach-picnic Mediterranean food, and Tavola on Robson Street near Denman, known for hand-made pasta.
