Praia dos Pescadores is a 225-meter beach at the center of Albufeira's old town, on the Algarve coast of southern Portugal, about 0.2 km from the town center itself. The name means Fishermen's Beach: before Albufeira built a harbor, this sand was the fishing fleet's working base, boats hauled up between trips. Today the beach is covered in licensed parasol and lounger concessions instead, one of the clearest examples on the Algarve of a resort economy built directly on top of a fishing economy that collapsed decades earlier.

## What to Expect

The beach is small and it fills fast: 225 meters long, 106 meters wide at low tide, bounded by a cliff headland toward Praia do Túnel to the west and a concrete pier over the Ribeira de Albufeira to the east, past which sits Praia do Inatel. Access is at street level from the old town's Avenida 25 de Abril, or by an outdoor escalator that descends from the Pau da Bandeira clifftop, where a second car park sits above Bairro dos Pescadores, the "Fishermen's Neighborhood" of white houses that backs onto the sand.

The water carries an official rating, not just a marketing claim: EU Bathing Water classification PTCV7X rates it "1 - excellent" under the EU Bathing Water Directive. The beach held Blue Flag status, the international eco-label covering water quality, safety, and environmental management, through 2025 but was dropped from the 2026 Bandeira Azul list. Lifeguards patrol mid-May through mid-October; outside that window there is no coverage. Restrooms and showers are available on-site, and no entrance fee applies.

## Most Relevant Lenses

**History** — The beach's name records a fishing economy that no longer operates here in any visible form. Albufeira ran five canning factories employing 700-800 people in the early 20th century; between 1930 and 1960 that industry collapsed and the town's population fell by half, before 1960s tourism rebuilt the town on the same waterfront.

**Family** — Shallow entry, a moderate low-tide width, lifeguards in summer, and food available immediately behind the beach make this a workable choice for families, though space is tight in July and August.

**Photography** — The cliff faces on both ends of the beach take on strong ochre tones in late-afternoon light, and the escalator and Bairro dos Pescadores' tiered white houses give the beach a distinct backdrop compared to open coastline.

**Culture** — Every New Year's Eve, a temporary stage on the adjacent Largo 25 de Abril hosts concerts — UB40 played a New Year's Eve concert there at the turn of 2009/2010 (accounts differ on whether the date fell in 2009 or 2010) — followed by fireworks fired from boats moored just offshore, the one night a year vessels return to this stretch of water.

## Practical Context

Praia dos Pescadores is a working example of a beach whose name and current use point in different directions. It earned the name Fishermen's Beach doing exactly that; it has kept the name through an earthquake, an industrial collapse, and a tourism boom that turned the same sand into one of the busiest stretches on the Algarve coast.
