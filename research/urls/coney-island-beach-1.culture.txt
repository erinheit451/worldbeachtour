# notes: Wikipedia "Coney Island in popular culture" is the hub for film/music/lit (Annie Hall, Warriors, Requiem, Wonder Wheel, "Coney Island Baby" by The Excellents 1962 vs Lou Reed 1975/76 album).
#  Little Fugitive (1953, Morris Engel): film-foundation + Brooklyn Mag. Ferlinghetti 1958 New Directions, title phrase from Henry Miller's Black Spring (Wikipedia).
#  Polar Bear Club: founded 1903 by Bernarr Macfadden (Atlas Obscura; claims oldest US winter bathing club). Mermaid Parade first held 1983; CIUSA founded 1980 by Dick Zigun (NYC Go, amNY).
#  Nathan's: 1916, Nathan Handwerker, $300 (company history + Wikipedia). Totonno's: 1924 (own site). Ruby's: opened 1934, evicted ~2010-2011 amid city/Zamperla lease (Brooklyn Paper, Village Voice).
#  Weegee 1940 beach photo at V&A; Brooklyn Museum 2008 show covers Stella, Marsh, Arbus, Weegee, Evans.
#  Contradictions: Polar Bear plunge numbering (MCNY called 2019 the 115th; others call 2022 the 119th). Hot dog contest "1916" origin is a widely repeated myth (Nathan's own history does not support it; the 1916 story was a PR invention per commonly cited reporting - verify). Singing waiters Cantor/Durante story absent from company history.
#  Recency: Ruby's sources are 2010-2011; Nathan's Smithfield deal reported Jan 2026 in search summary - verify. America Magazine and Jerusalem Post pieces are undated/2018.
