# one in metropolitan Melbourne (designated 1983 under the Nudity (Prescribed Areas) Act). Real political
# tension: Mornington Peninsula Shire Council moved in Feb 2022 to canvass revoking the status; a 26-year
# regular (named "Michael" in SBS coverage, gay, describes the beach as important to the LGBTIQ+ community) led
# a public campaign; community consultation found ~92% of ~4,000 respondents wanted to keep clothing-optional
# status; council voted Dec 6 to retain it, while also asking the state government for funding for car-park
# surveillance cameras, police monitoring, signage, toilets and access-track repair — ongoing infrastructure
# neglect (MPNews 2021 and 2023 pieces) is a second, quieter tension thread. Historical layer: the beach is
# named for an 1867 mansion, "Sunnyside" (built by London merchant Francis Gillett, later "Morning Star"), whose
