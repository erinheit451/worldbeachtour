# expected tension: the scaffold frames Cala Salada as an untouched, low-crowd wild escape ("solitude," "narrow access road naturally constrains crowds"). Reality is a MANAGED tourist bottleneck: private-car congestion got bad enough that Sant Antoni/Consell d'Eivissa launched "Platja Connectada" in 2016 (first such program on Ibiza) — free bus + capacity-gated parking — explicitly because vehicle overflow was blocking emergency access (fire, police, ambulance) in a zone rated extreme wildfire risk. Car access has been actively restricted/controlled every summer season since (May 1–Oct 15, 9:30–17:00 gated access points) — this is the real story: seclusion is now infrastructure-enforced, not organic, and 2025 visitor reviews describe full car parks by 11am and crowding with rental hammocks. Use this as the central "tension" beat rather than the scaffold's soft "solitude" framing.
